Security and sovereignty

Built for the most scrutinised data a company holds.

Finance data is regulated, audited and sensitive by nature. Ygdra is designed so that you can see, control and prove everything that happens to it.

  • European by default

    Ygdra runs in European regions, and can be deployed inside your own cloud or data centre when the data cannot leave it.

  • Sealed agent sandboxes

    Agents do their work in isolated sandboxes with no route to the outside world. Outbound network access is denied at the operating system level, not by policy alone.

  • Every action on the record

    Each read, transformation, decision and approval is written to an append-only record for the project. You can replay how any result came to be.

  • Signed evidence

    Evidence packs are signed, so anyone can verify later that nothing in them changed after the fact.

  • Least access to source systems

    Connections to SAP use the narrowest access the task needs. Reading a system and writing to one are separate, explicit permissions.

  • Your data is yours

    Customer data is used to do the work you ask for and nothing else. Knowledge built on a project stays with that project.

For your auditors

Evidence your auditors can work with.

The evidence pack is designed around how auditors test a data migration: completeness, accuracy and the controls around judgment. Source rows, reconciliations, decisions and approvals are linked, so a sample can be followed from source to target balance.
