Migrate

Leave legacy ERP with the books intact.

Ygdra Migrate moves finance from SAP ECC to S/4HANA with agents that read every record, reconcile every ledger and put every decision on the record, from the first mock load to cutover.

The last mile

Programmes do not stall on mapping.

They stall in the weeks before cutover, when the mock loads almost balance and someone reconciles by hand at midnight before a steering committee.

Ygdra moves reconciliation from the end of the programme to every step of it. A transformation only counts as done when its totals are conserved. When they are not, the difference is named to the account, the document and the amount, and put in front of the person who can decide.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Agents read the full estate: ledgers, open items, master data and the custom fields nobody documented. You get a plain account of what is there and what state it is in.

  2. 02

    Design

    Source is mapped to the target design, field by field. Where the mapping needs judgment, the question comes to your team with the evidence attached.

  3. 03

    Cleanse and transform

    Every rule is written as code you can read, run and rerun. Cleansing that would change the meaning of a figure is flagged, never applied silently.

  4. 04

    Reconcile

    Totals are tied out per account, per currency and per company code, on every run. Differences are traced to the document that caused them.

  5. 05

    Cut over

    Load-ready files and a signed manifest go to your load tooling, together with the evidence pack your auditors and controllers sign against.

Every decision on the record

Which account a custom field belongs to. Whether period 13 postings go into the opening balance. Each call is recorded once with its evidence and approver, then reused on every run that follows.

Built for the whole estate

Many company codes, many currencies, many entities. Agents work across them in parallel, and every result ties back to one reconciliation you can hand to finance.

Walk into cutover with the proof already in hand.
