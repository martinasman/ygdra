How it works

Agents do the reading.
People make the calls.

  1. 01

    Read everything

    Agents connect to the source and read every table, document and posting. Nothing is sampled, so nothing is assumed.

  2. 02

    Decide in the open

    Every judgment call comes to a person with its evidence attached. Decisions are recorded once and reused on every run.

  3. 03

    Prove the result

    Work only counts as done when it passes its checks. Totals are conserved, differences are named, and the proof is signed.
