Built in Stockholm for the systems that run the enterprise.

Why we exist

Ygdra takes its name from Yggdrasil, the tree in Norse myth that holds the worlds together. Enterprise systems play the same part inside a company: everything else rests on them.

Changing those systems has always been slow, expensive and uncertain, staffed by large teams working from samples and spreadsheets. We think AI can change that, but only if its work can be checked. So we build agents that read everything, leave every judgment to people, and prove what they did.

We started with the hardest version of the problem: moving the finance core of a European enterprise from one ERP to another, under the eye of its auditors. Everything we build is held to that standard.

What we believe

Proof over confidence

We do not quote accuracy scores. We show the work, and we build so that anyone can check it.

People own judgment

Agents do the reading and the checking. The decisions that shape a business stay with the people accountable for it.

Read everything

Sampling was a budget decision. With agents, full coverage is the default, and we hold ourselves to it.

Quiet, durable software

Enterprise systems outlive trends. We build tools that are calm to use and still make sense in ten years.
